# The Morning Roundup

A wizarding-style daily newspaper, printed fresh every morning at 08:00 IST from the RSS feeds of BBC News, The New York Times, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, The Hindu, Times of India, The Verge, ScienceDaily and ESPN.

**Read it:** https://10priyankasinghofficial-code.github.io/morning-roundup/

- `build.py` fetches the feeds and renders `template.html` into `site/index.html`
- `.github/workflows/daily.yml` runs it daily and deploys to GitHub Pages

Headlines and photographs belong to their publishers; every story links to the original.
